# Public Evidence Policy

## Safe to publish

- Aggregate metrics already approved for resume use
- Architecture diagrams containing no private endpoints or credentials
- Synthetic examples that reproduce the workflow without customer data
- Sanitized screenshots with names, contact details, IDs, and commercial data removed
- Evaluation methodology, test structure, and non-proprietary schemas

## Requires written employer approval

- Any Funded Nation or Evore Labs screenshot, report, architecture diagram, or sample output
- Company logos used as project evidence
- Statements that an internal prototype was adopted, deployed, or used operationally
- Employee/user counts, efficiency estimates, and aggregate record counts

## Never publish

- Customer conversations, CRM exports, call audio, phone numbers, email addresses, or PII
- Proprietary alpha definitions, asset universes, signal parameters, P&L, or research logic
- Private source code, credentials, internal URLs, pricing, or contractual information
- Materials that imply a prototype was autonomous, production-grade, or live trading when it was not
